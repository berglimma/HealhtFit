HealthFit 1.0.11 (24)

Resolution for Guideline 3.1.1 (In-App Purchase):
- The shipping App Store binary does NOT offer in-app courtesy/promo code unlocks.
- 30-day QA vouchers exist only in TestFlight/Debug builds (hidden and blocked in App Store builds).
- Production paid access is StoreKit / App Store Offer Codes only.

Resolution for Guideline 3.1.2 (Subscriptions):
- Paywall now shows the billed price first (e.g. annual total /ano); monthly equivalent only as secondary “equiv. a …/mês” in the same currency as StoreKit.
- Purchase button includes the exact StoreKit price to be charged.
- Functional https links on the paywall (and Meu plano): Privacy Policy, Terms of Use (EULA — Apple standard), and HealthFit Terms.
- Full auto-renewal disclosure: charged to Apple ID at confirmation; renews unless cancelled ≥24h before period end; renewal charged within 24h of end; manage/cancel in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
- Restore Purchases and Manage Subscription remain on the paywall and Profile → Meu plano.
- Subscription title, length (1 month / 1 year), and price are listed above the disclosure.

How to verify:
1. Profile → Meu plano → Ver planos e assinar
2. Confirm annual cards show “…/ano” as the primary price
3. Scroll under Assinar: Restaurar compras, Privacy/EULA/Termos links, and the full renewal text
4. Confirm there is NO “código de cortesia” button on the App Store build
