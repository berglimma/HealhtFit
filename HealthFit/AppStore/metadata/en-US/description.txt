HealthFit — Your smart personal trainer

Train smarter, track your progress, and take care of your health in one place.

KEY FEATURES
• Personalized workouts by body type and goal (hypertrophy, fat loss, maintenance)
• Demo GIFs for every exercise
• Cardio: running, walking, cycling, swimming, and more
• Advanced modalities: Surf, Kitesurf, Rowing, Climbing, and Combat sports (fight timer)
• AI Assistant with check-ins, tips, and daily motivation
• Meal plan with shopping list
• Duo team training with chat
• Kite Spot Buddy: friend radar and help alert for kitesurf (opt-in, session-only)
• Apple Health integration (heart rate, calories, steps, sleep/HRV when authorized)
• Rest timer between sets
• Progress reports and body evolution
• Apple Watch companion
• Water, workout, and rest reminders
• Optional plans (Basic, Fit, AI Plus, Complete) in Profile → My Plan

WHO IT'S FOR
Beginner and intermediate athletes who want guided training, progress tracking, and consistency — with or without a personal trainer.

IMPORTANT
HealthFit provides informational guidance only. It does not replace medical evaluation, a registered dietitian, or professional coaching. Consult a physician before starting any exercise program.
