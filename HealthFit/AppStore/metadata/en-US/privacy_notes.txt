Data collected and purpose (for the App Privacy questionnaire):

1. Email — account creation and password recovery
2. Name — personalization and greetings
3. Profile photo (optional) — display on profile
4. Body evolution photos (optional) — private progress comparison and reports; not sold or auto-published
5. Health/fitness data — workouts, sleep, hydration, HealthKit (with user permission)
6. User ID — Firebase sync

Body evolution photos are confidential and account-bound; removed with account deletion and when a completed evolution cycle drops prior photos per app logic.
Data is NOT used for tracking across apps.
Account deletion is available in the app (Profile → Delete Account).
